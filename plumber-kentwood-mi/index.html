Is the blockage in your house line or the main line?

One backing-up fixture usually points at a branch line that drain cleaning can clear. When the lowest drain in the house backs up and others gurgle, the blockage is often farther along the building sewer, and you are into sewer line repair territory.

The property line is the key dividing point. The pipe on your lot is normally yours to repair, while the public main belongs to the utility. If neighbors are backing up too, call the utility first, and then call us for any damage on your side.

Signs the sewer line needs more than a snake

Several of these together suggest a line problem, not a simple clog.

Repeat clogs

A drain that clears and then fails again within weeks often has a bellied, cracked or root-invaded section ahead of the snake.

Several drains gurgle

If a flushed toilet makes the tub bubble, air and water are fighting for space in a blocked main.

Soggy yard patch

A wet, sunken or unusually green strip over the line can mean the pipe has separated underground.

Sewer odor outside

A steady smell near the cleanout or along the foundation is a sign wastewater is escaping before it reaches the main.

Repair now or camera first

Camera inspection first

  • Shows the actual break, offset or root mass.
  • Helps decide between spot repair and replacing a longer run.
  • Is worth doing before any digging.

Straight to repair

  • Fits when the failure is already obvious, such as a collapsed pipe at the cleanout.
  • Saves a trip if the line is easy to reach.
  • Still benefits from a quick look with a camera afterward.

What to do at home while dispatch finds your plumber

Four quick actions protect the house during an after-hours call.

Close the main

Turn the main water valve clockwise. If there is no main or it will not budge, close the fixture shutoff and say so on the phone.

Power down wet zones

Flip breakers for the basement or garage only from dry footing. If the panel stands in water, leave it and tell us.

Clear the space

Pull stored items and rugs away from the drain, heater or wall. We need room around the cleanout, which is often near the foundation.
