Plumbing in Wyoming, MI: what to expect

Wyoming grew fast after the war, and much of Wyoming was built in the 1950s boom, so many homes now have original pipes and heaters at the end of their life. Cast iron drains clog with scale, galvanized supply lines lose pressure, and original water heaters are long overdue. If your home is from that era, a camera look at the drain and a check of the supply piping is often smarter than patching one leak at a time.

We serve homes and light commercial sites across the 49509 and 49519 ZIP codes, from the 28th Street corridor to the GUB neighborhoods and Godwin Heights. Sewer backups in the city are special: Wyoming Public Works handles the city side of the sewer, so call them first at 616-530-7260, then call us if the problem is on your side of the property line.

Water in Wyoming comes from Lake Michigan and is moderately hard, so water heaters and tankless units collect scale. City plumbing permits, which follow the 2021 Michigan Plumbing Code based on the IPC, cover hot water heater replacement, and we can explain what that means for your job when you call.

The calls we take most

Most overnight calls fall into a few groups. A water heater tank lets go and floods a basement utility room. A kitchen or main drain stops moving and water backs up into a tub. A pipe freezes in an exterior wall during a Michigan cold snap and splits when it thaws. A toilet runs over because the line behind it is blocked.

Each of these has a first step you can take before we arrive. Shut off the water at the fixture or the main, stop using drains if sewage is involved, and move anything valuable out of the wet area. When you call, we walk through those steps with you and send a plumber.

Planned work gets the same care. Replacing a water heater before it fails, repiping a section of old galvanized line, or fixing a faucet that has dripped for months all go on the schedule, and same-day appointments are available when the calendar allows.

Not sure it is an emergency?

If water is flowing, sewage is backing up, or you smell gas, treat it as urgent. For gas, leave the home and call 911 or your gas utility first. For everything else, the 24/7 line can help you decide whether it can wait for a same-day appointment.
