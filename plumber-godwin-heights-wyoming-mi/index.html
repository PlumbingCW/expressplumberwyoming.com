Godwin Heights sits around Division Avenue and 36th Street in the City of Wyoming. The Wyoming history room lists it among the city's historic neighborhoods, and the Godwin name goes back to Augustus Godwin, an early pioneer and county supervisor.

If you own a home here, the first plumbing question is often not how to fix it but who is responsible for it. This page explains that split, then covers the older-home issues worth checking and the permit rules. To book a visit, call (855) 575-2890.

Who handles a sewer backup in Godwin Heights

Wyoming Public Works accepts responsibility for the city sewer mains and the connections within the right of way, and says to call them before a plumber for a sewer backup at 616-530-7260. If the blockage is on the private side, you hire and pay a plumber.

That rule comes from the city's own Public Works page and applies to every Wyoming address. In practice, if several drains back up at once or the cleanout outside is full, call Public Works first. If only one fixture is slow, a plumber can usually find the cause inside the house.

Older-home checks for Godwin Heights

Division Avenue was improved to a plank road in 1854, and the streets around it were platted in stages through the 1920s. Listing sites describe mostly ranch homes today, so ages vary from block to block. These are general checks, not findings about any one house.

Slow main drain

A drain that gurgles or clears and returns can point to a partial blockage in the line. A sewer camera inspection shows what is in the pipe instead of guessing.

Old shutoff valves

Valves that have not moved in years can stick or leak when you finally turn them. Test the main shutoff once a year so you know it works before a leak.

Aging water heater

A tank that is past its usual life can leak without warning. Rust at the base or damp floor nearby is a reason to book a look soon.

Hard water scale

Lake Michigan water in this area tests moderately hard, so scale builds up inside heaters and on fixtures over time.

Permits for common Godwin Heights jobs

Wyoming Building Inspections (616-530-7285) requires a plumbing permit before work starts on hot water heaters and on replacing drain, vent and water supply pipes.

Michigan requires the work to be done by a licensed person for that type of work, unless the owner lives in the home. You can check a plumber's license on the state LARA lookup before you hire. Ask who pulls the permit before you agree to a job.
